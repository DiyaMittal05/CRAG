# Data Export & Deletion

## Exporting your data
You can export all your data as a ZIP file (CSV + JSON) from Settings > Data
> Export Data. Exports are generated asynchronously; you'll get an email with
a download link once it's ready, typically within 15 minutes. Download links
expire after 7 days.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent and removes all associated data, including
projects, uploaded files, and billing history, after a 14-day grace period
during which you can cancel the deletion by logging back in. Initiate
deletion from Settings > Account > Delete Account.

## Data retention
Deleted projects are retained in a recoverable state for 30 days before
permanent removal. Backups are retained for 90 days for disaster-recovery
purposes only and are not accessible through the standard export flow.

## GDPR / data requests
EU users can request a full data export or erasure under GDPR by emailing
privacy@example.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.
