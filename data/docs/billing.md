# Billing & Subscriptions

## How billing cycles work
Subscriptions renew monthly on the date you first subscribed. You are charged
automatically using the payment method on file. Invoices are emailed within
24 hours of each renewal and are also available under Settings > Billing >
Invoice History.

## Changing or canceling a plan
You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from Settings >
Billing > Manage Plan. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of
the current billing cycle; you keep access to your current plan's features
until then. There are no cancellation fees.

## Refund policy
Refunds are issued for duplicate charges or billing errors on our side within
30 days of the charge. We do not offer prorated refunds for mid-cycle
downgrades or voluntary cancellations. To request a refund, contact support
with your invoice number.

## Failed payments
If a renewal payment fails, we retry three times over seven days. During this
window your account stays active but shows a "Payment Due" banner. If all
retries fail, the account is downgraded to the free tier until payment is
updated.
