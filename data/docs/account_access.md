# Account Access

## Resetting your password
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password." Enter the email address on
your account and we'll send a reset link that expires after 1 hour. If you
don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder and
confirm the email matches the one on your account.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
2FA can be enabled under Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps
(TOTP) and SMS codes. If you lose access to your 2FA device, use one of the
backup codes generated when you first enabled 2FA, or contact support to
verify your identity and disable 2FA on your account.

## Account lockouts
After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes as a
security measure. This is automatic and resolves itself; there is no need to
contact support unless the lockout persists after 15 minutes.

## Changing your email address
Update your email under Settings > Profile > Email. You'll need to confirm
the change via a link sent to the new address before it takes effect.
